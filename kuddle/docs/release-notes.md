# Kuddle release notes

## 0.1.0

The first Kuddle: your home rig for clean, warm bass, as a VST3 for Ableton (and a standalone app).

- **The chain**, in playing order: Mute (flatwound and foam feel), Auto wah (Quack's engine, darker and more vocal, lows kept dry), Valve DI (always on), Opto, Tape, Tone and Room (small dark room or spring, off to start with).
- **Melt sweep** on the expression pedal: heel open and clear, toe warmer and darker (more mute, more tape, the treble rolling off). Melt reach on the Deep page sets how far each part moves. With the wah in manual, the pedal works the wah instead. The pedal can also work the wah, volume or room mix.
- **Footswitches**, the same pattern as Keystone: tap A Mute, B Auto wah, C Room, D Tape push. Hold A previous preset, B next preset, C tuner, D Squeeze. Hold C and D for Keep. Read straight from the footswitch's cable, no MIDI track needed.
- **15 named presets** shared by every copy of Kuddle, starting with Voodoo, Khruangbin and Dub.
- **Main, Deep and Pedal pages** in the brown and orange Flip Fella look, with Pebble in shades and a pork pie hat.
- **Tuner** on a hold of C, accurate to a tenth of a cent on bass notes, output muted while it's up.
- **Keeper** writes to the shared Flip Fella Keepers folder, tagged with the Kuddle rig.
- Latency 63 samples (1.3 ms at 48 kHz), the same whatever is switched on.
