KUDDLE
Your home rig: clean, warm bass for hip-hop, soul and jazz.

INSTALLING
Run Kuddle-Setup.exe. It puts Kuddle.vst3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3,
where Ableton looks for plugins, and a standalone Kuddle app in the Start menu.
In Ableton, open Preferences, Plug-ins, and press Rescan if Kuddle doesn't show up.

USING IT IN ABLETON
1. Put Kuddle on the audio track your bass comes in on.
2. Footswitches: Kuddle reads your footswitch straight from its USB cable, so there's
   nothing to route. In Ableton's Preferences, Link, Tempo and MIDI, turn Track and
   Remote off for the footswitch's input, so Ableton doesn't hold onto it.
   If you'd rather send the footswitch through Ableton: make a MIDI track, set its input
   to the footswitch and its output to the Kuddle track. Then on Kuddle's Pedal page,
   set Footswitch input to "Off: footswitch MIDI comes from Ableton".
3. The expression pedal works the Melt sweep. On the Pedal page, rock it to the heel
   and press Heel, then to the toe and press Toe.

FOOTSWITCHES
Tap A Mute, B Auto wah, C Room, D Tape push.
Hold A previous preset, B next preset, C tuner, D Squeeze (firmer compression).
Hold C and D: Keep. Any tap leaves the tuner.

PRESETS
Click the preset name for the list, Save in, Rename and Clear. Up to 15, kept in
AppData\Roaming\Kevin Kaye\Kuddle Presets.xml, so every copy of Kuddle has them.

KEEPER
Press Keep (or hold C and D) when you hear something you love. Kuddle saves the
settings and the last 8 seconds of audio to Documents\Flip Fella Keepers, alongside
your other Flip Fella plugins.
